Ayaka Tsutsui

ayakatsutsui1128 [at] digitalnature.slis.tsukuba.ac.jp

I am a Ph.D. student at the University of Tsukuba, advised by Yoichi Ochiai. I am also a part-time researcher at Miraikan (The National Museum of Emerging Science and Innovation), where I work with Chieko Asakawa and Hironobu Takagi.

My research lies at the intersection of human-computer interaction, accessibility, wearable devices, and interaction design.

I received my B.S. from the College of Knowledge and Library Sciences (KLIS) at the University of Tsukuba and my M.S. from the Graduate School of Comprehensive Human Sciences, University of Tsukuba, both under the supervision of Yoichi Ochiai.
